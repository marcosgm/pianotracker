How It Works

  1. Register: Create an account with your email and password
  2. Log Session: After a practice session, select the type (Chords, Scales, Course, Songs), add optional tempo (for Chords/Scales), and document what you played
  3. Automatic Routines: The app remembers your practice combinations and offers them next time for quick selection
  4. Track Progress: View your complete session history with statistics and filter by practice type
